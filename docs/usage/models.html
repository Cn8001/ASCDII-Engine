Guides

Models

Wavefront .obj models from Blender or any other 3D tool drop straight into a scene and draw in Braille with lighting, shadows and fog, at any rotation.

Importing

  1. Export your model as .obj, with materials (.mtl) if it has any.
  2. Drag the .obj into the Scene view. It is copied to models/ together with its .mtl files and the textures they name, and placed where you dropped it.
  3. Place more copies from GameObject, Model, by dragging the tile from the Project window, or by double clicking it.

What is read

OBJ / MTLUsed for
v, fPositions and faces; faces with more than three corners are split into triangles.
vtTexture coordinates, so textured models look as they do in your 3D tool.
usemtl, mtllibWhich material each part uses.
KdThe part's color.
map_KdThe part's texture, imported as a texture asset.

Normals in the file are not needed: faces are shaded flat, which reads best at Braille resolution. Keep models simple; a few hundred faces are plenty for a building.

Import settings

Click a model tile to see them in the Inspector:

SettingDoes
upZOff for the usual Y up export. Turn it on for files that are already Z up, as some CAD tools write them.
scaleWorld units per file unit. One world unit is one tile of ground; a door is about 2 units tall.

The model is turned so that its front, as seen in your 3D tool's front view, faces the default camera.

The Mesh component

PropertyDoes
modelWhich model to draw.
materialOptional; replaces the model's own materials on every part.
tint, emissiveColor multiplied over the model, and self lighting.
twoSidedDraw back faces too, for open models or ones with mixed up face directions.
shadowA soft contact shadow on the ground.

The object's rotation, scale and flip all apply, and collision uses the rotated bounds when the object is solid.
