Audio

Sounds are WAV and MP3 assets, played by Audio Source components and by scripts, in the editor's play mode and in built games.

Sound files

Sound files dragged into the Project window are imported into the open folder. A double click on a sound plays it. WAV files may be 8 or 16 bit, mono or stereo, at any sample rate; MP3 files of any common sample rate are supported. Sounds are converted to 44.1 kHz stereo when loaded.

Audio Source

Add an Audio Source with Add Component and drag a sound onto its clip slot.

PropertyMeaning
clipThe sound.
volumeVolume from 0 to 1; changes apply to a playing sound.
loopRestarts the sound at its end, for music and ambience.
playOnStartPlays the sound when the game starts; off for sounds started by scripts.

From scripts

function key(self, k)
  if k == "e" then
    local door = self:component("audio_source")
    door.volume = 0.6
    door:play()
  end
end

-- a one shot without a component
play_sound("audio/coin.wav", 0.8)

The Audio Source's methods are play(), stop() and playing(); every property is a field. play_sound(path, volume) plays a sound once without a component. See the Lua API.

OutputIn the editor, sounds play through the editor window. A built game mixes its sounds and plays them on the system's sound device, through ALSA on Linux. Without a sound device, the game runs without sound.
