Guides

Audio

Drop .wav and .mp3 files into the project, put an Audio Source on an object, and the sound plays in the editor's play mode and in built games, through the computer's speakers.

Sound files

Drag sound files into the Project window; they land in audio/. Double click a sound to hear it. WAV files may be 8 or 16 bit, mono or stereo, at any rate; MP3 files of any common rate work too.

Audio Source

Add an Audio Source with Add Component and drag a sound onto its clip slot.

PropertyDoes
clipThe sound to play.
volume0 to 1. Changing it while the sound plays fades it.
loopStart over at the end, for music and ambience.
playOnStartPlay when the game starts. Turn it off to play from a script.

From scripts

function key(self, k)
  if k == "e" then
    local door = self:component("audio_source")
    door.volume = 0.6
    door:play()
  end
end

-- a one shot without a component
play_sound("audio/coin.wav", 0.8)

play(), stop() and playing() are the Audio Source's methods; every property is a field. See the Lua API.

Where sound comes fromIn the editor the sounds play through the editor window. A built game mixes them itself and plays them on the system's sound device; on Linux that is ALSA, present on almost every desktop. Without a sound device the game runs silent instead of failing.
